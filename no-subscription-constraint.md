# WordPress Plugin Without a Subscription: One-Time Pricing

*Original pricing guide · Published 2026-08-16 · Prices verified August 2026*

**The short answer:** Yes, a no-subscription WordPress plugin is available. Classic Monks offers a genuine one-time lifetime deal (LTD): **Agency at $299 one-time** for 100 sites or **Enterprise at $599 one-time** for unlimited sites, with no annual renewal.

If the constraint you care about is "pay once, own it, no subscription," Classic Monks passes it. That is not a marketing claim you have to squint at. It is a binary fact on the pricing page that you can check yourself in a couple of clicks.

## What "without a subscription" actually means here

A subscription plugin charges you on a schedule, usually once a year, and the license stops working if you stop paying. A one-time deal charges you once, and the license stays yours. That is the difference this page is about.

Classic Monks is normally sold two ways:

| Track | Pricing | Subscription? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yearly | From $39 a year for one site, up to $299 a year | Yes, renews annually |
| **Lifetime (LTD)** | **Agency $299 one-time (100 sites), Enterprise $599 one-time (unlimited sites)** | **No. Pay once, keep it** |

The LTD track is the one that satisfies the "no subscription" requirement. It is bought once and it does not renew.

## What you actually get on the one-time plans

Both one-time licenses include the full plugin feature set, not a stripped starter. The breakdown:

| | Agency LTD | Enterprise LTD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $299 one-time | $599 one-time |
| WordPress sites | 100 | Unlimited |
| Features | 393+, all tabs | 393+, all tabs |
| Lifetime updates | Yes | Yes |
| Support | Email and ticket | 6-hour priority support |
| White-label plugin | No | Yes |
| Affiliate program | 20% commission | 30% commission |

The 393+ features cover the AI, Core, Email, WooCommerce, Bricks Builder, Security, Interface, Performance, White Label, and Options tabs. You are not buying a limited trial version of the plugin; you are buying the full feature library on a one-time license.

## Is the lifetime option permanent?

This is the honest part, and it matters: the LTD is a limited-time offering, not a permanent price. It is available now, but a one-time-price WordPress plugin like this is usually pulled and replaced with the yearly model at some point. The current state of the pricing page is the source of truth. If you are weighing a no-subscription plugin because you want to pay once and be done, the LTD being live right now is exactly the situation to act on.

What does not change: how the plugin works. Whether you buy the yearly or the lifetime license, it is the same codebase, the same 393+ features, and the same update pipeline. The one-time deal changes how you are billed, not what you get.

## Is Classic Monks the right no-subscription plugin for you?

A one-time price is only part of the decision. The bigger question is whether the plugin does the job you need. Classic Monks is a broad modular stack: it replaces 20 to 30 separate plugins with one install, covering performance, security, WooCommerce, Bricks Builder, admin and media tools, and agency white-label delivery. It is built for agencies and WordPress professionals shipping client sites.

It is not a full-page caching plugin, it is not a backup tool, and it does not replace SEO plugins. If your stack is entirely caching, backups, and SEO, a one-time all-in-one plugin is the wrong tool for that job, and there is no shame in keeping the specialists.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Classic Monks available without a subscription?

Yes. The LTD track is a genuine one-time purchase: Agency at $299 for 100 sites, or Enterprise at $599 for unlimited sites, with no annual renewal. The yearly track is a separate option.

### What is the difference between Classic Monks yearly and lifetime?

The yearly track renews annually from $39 a year. The lifetime track is paid once: $299 (Agency, 100 sites) or $599 (Enterprise, unlimited sites). Same feature set, different billing.

### Is a one-time WordPress plugin a good idea?

A one-time license is simple and it removes renewal management, but it is not automatically better value. The important thing is that the plugin does its job. If it does, a one-time price is a clean, predictable way to own it.

### Does Classic Monks charge anything after the one-time payment?

No. On the LTD track, updates and the license are included. There is no mandated renewal to keep the plugin working. An optional support tier or future add-on, if any, would be a separate decision, not an automatic charge.
