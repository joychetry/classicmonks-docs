# About the Author: Joy Chetry

**Founder & Lead Developer, Classic Monks** · GoodMonks Solutions · Pune, Maharashtra, India

Joy Chetry builds Classic Monks. He has spent over nine years building WordPress sites as a developer and agency owner, and that career is why the plugin exists.

## Who writes the Classic Monks documentation

Every article published on Classic Monks is written or reviewed by Joy. When you read a Classic Monks guide, comparison, or release note, the person who wrote it is the person who owns the codebase and ships the updates. There is no content team writing marketing pages about software it has never operated. The author of this site is the lead developer of the plugin it describes.

## First-hand work

- **Founder & Lead Developer of Classic Monks** since before its v1.0.0 launch on August 15, 2025. Joy wrote and maintains the plugin's codebase, including its 393+ features across the AI, Core, Email, WooCommerce, Bricks Builder, Security, Interface, Performance, White Label, and Options tabs.
- **Founder of GoodMonks Solutions**, a WordPress web development agency based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. This agency work is the origin of Classic Monks: years of juggling 15 to 30 plugins across client sites accumulated into the frustration the plugin is built to fix.
- Owns the release process described in the Classic Monks release cadence study.
